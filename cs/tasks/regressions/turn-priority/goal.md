An interactive conversation turn must start within seconds, not wait behind lane jobs (2026-11-12: a client-web turn waited about three 60-second ticks behind two Codex lanes, then ran in 10 s). The node admits conversation turns ahead of lane, scheduled and batch jobs (`ops/bin/_turn_priority.py`, used by `tick`), and node-listen retries a pending turn every second instead of every 15. A batch job waiting at least five minutes still gets the next slot, so turns cannot starve lanes. The vitals guard still holds: under critical memory pressure nothing is admitted.
